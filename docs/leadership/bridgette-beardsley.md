# Bridgette Beardsley

## Co-Founder & Chief Technology and Strategy Officer

**LuxSync LLC**  
**Leadership profile:** Approved for website and company materials  
**Brand system:** LuxSync v3  
**Voice:** Intelligent Calm

## Website Biography

Bridgette Beardsley is the Co-Founder and visionary behind LuxSync, a premium intelligent-living company created to make smart-home technology feel elegant, understandable, and purposeful.

With more than three decades of experience in enterprise technology and operations, Bridgette brings extensive leadership expertise in complex technology platforms, automation, reliability, governance, process design, and customer-focused transformation. She founded LuxSync around a simple conviction: people should not have to become technology experts to enjoy a safer, smarter, and more comfortable environment.

As Chief Technology and Strategy Officer, Bridgette leads LuxSync's technology direction, business strategy, digital customer experience, financial systems, compliance, operational governance, and future platform development. Her approach shapes everything from curated SmartThings-compatible solutions to guided recommendation experiences that begin with how customers want to live—not with a list of devices.

Her vision for LuxSync is intelligent luxury made effortless: thoughtfully selected technology that works quietly in the background while helping homes, rental properties, and professional spaces feel more secure, comfortable, and beautifully connected.

## Compact Biography

Bridgette Beardsley is the Co-Founder and visionary behind LuxSync. Drawing on more than three decades of enterprise technology and operations leadership, she guides the company's technology, strategy, governance, and digital customer experience. Her vision is to make intelligent living feel elegant and effortless by replacing technical complexity with trusted curation, thoughtful automation, and solutions designed around how people want to live.

## Brand Presentation

- Use the protected LuxSync logo artwork without redrawing, retyping, recoloring, or regenerating it.
- Set headings and title treatments in Manrope 500/600.
- Set biography and supporting copy in Inter 400/500.
- Use only the approved LuxSync v3 palette for branded text, rules, backgrounds, and accents.
- Champagne Rose Gold Metallic may be used as a restrained premium accent with `#D6B0A0` as its anchor.
- Keep the presentation spacious, refined, warm, and confident.
