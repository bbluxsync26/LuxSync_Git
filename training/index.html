      Why SmartThings PWA — Not a Native App

      Apple charges 15–30% on all in-app subscription revenue through the App Store. A native app on iOS at $59/mo per property means $8.85–$17.70 per property goes to Apple — not LuxSync. The SmartThings PWA model eliminates this entirely. No App Store review cycles. No platform commission. Full UX control under the LuxSync brand. Instant updates without approval delays.
