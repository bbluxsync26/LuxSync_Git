# LuxSync Brand System 4.0

This is the authoritative visual asset library for LuxSync LLC. It replaces the flat v3 templates and all earlier generated logo approximations.

## Immutable logo artwork

- `01-logos/luxsync-monogram-approved.png` — exact approved LS orbit artwork
- `01-logos/luxsync-horizontal-approved.png` — exact approved horizontal lockup and slogan
- These files may be placed, resized proportionally, and centered on an approved background.
- They must not be retyped, redrawn, recolored, filtered, restyled, cropped, or regenerated.
- `logo-horizontal-llc.*` adds the legal entity beneath the unchanged approved lockup; it does not alter the wordmark.

## Approved palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Slate Navy | `#0D1526` | Primary dark background |
| Dark Suede | `#172036` | Elevated surfaces and cards |
| Pale Driftwood | `#D0BEB0` | Primary light text and warm surface |
| Warm Taupe Mauve | `#9E8B85` | Secondary text and neutral detail |
| Antique Rose Taupe | `#967878` | Restrained warm accent |
| Dusty Steel | `#7B96B2` | Cool technology accent |
| Champagne Rose Gold Metallic | Derived treatment | Dimensional metallic gradients anchored only in approved warm colors |

Lavender, purple, orange/copper drift, electric cyan, and neon colors are not part of this system.

## Typography

- Manrope 500/600: headings, navigation, button labels, and editable graphic UI.
- Inter 400/500: body copy, product information, forms, captions, and supporting UI.
- The logo lettering is protected artwork, not a system font.

## Asset families

- `00-reference/` — approved brand board
- `01-logos/` — protected logo masters, legal lockup, favicon, and responsive sizes
- `02-foundation/` — palette and typography references
- `03-ui/` — buttons, badges, forms, search, and commerce controls
- `04-icons/` — individual line icons and library board
- `05-heroes/` — website hero families and ROI guide artwork
- `06-ecommerce/` — category cards, product card, and trust bar
- `07-stationery/` — business cards, letterhead, invoice, and email signature
- `08-marketing/` — email header, social square, flyer, and presentation cover
- `09-documentation/` — branded documentation header

Every SVG master is paired with PNG and WebP output unless the file is a protected source logo or favicon.

## Visual standard

Use crisp photography, generous dark space, restrained metallic shine, and no more than one or two small peak-shine accents per composition. The orbit belongs only to the protected LS artwork; do not extend it behind long-form lettering or use it as a generic background decoration.
