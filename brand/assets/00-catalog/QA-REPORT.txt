Production-scene boundary
-------------------------
Production scenes are intentionally text-free. They must not contain baked-in:
- LuxSync website copy
- navigation
- prices or ratings
- buttons or CTAs
- stock/scarcity claims
- unapproved logo recreations

Website copy and controls should be native HTML/CSS or approved vector/raster brand
assets wherever practical.

Release validation
------------------
Before relying on the asset library for production:
1. Confirm exactly 97 SVG masters.
2. Confirm 97 non-scene PNGs and 97 non-scene WebPs.
3. Confirm six scene PNGs and six scene WebPs.
4. Confirm protected exact logo wrappers still reference approved logo rasters.
5. Confirm editable SVG text uses Manrope/Inter.
6. Confirm the canonical CSV dimensions match actual SVG dimensions.
7. Review the master contact sheet and representative light/dark usage.
8. Run scripts/validate-repository-consistency.py.
