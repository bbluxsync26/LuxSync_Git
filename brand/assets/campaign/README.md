# LuxSync Plush Drift Campaign Masters

Production-oriented, text-free raster masters for web, digital, print, and merchandise presentation. Apply the protected LuxSync logo and mutable copy as separate exact assets or live type. Never bake prices, guarantees, invented products, founder identities, account data, or unsupported claims into derivatives.

- `web/intelligent-living-master-v1.png` — wide web and digital campaign master.
- `print/plush-drift-cover-master-v1.png` — portrait cover master for guides, brochures, stories, and print.
- `merchandise/merchandise-blank-master-v1.png` — blank-placement merchandise presentation for controlled logo compositing.

Files under `brand/reference-boards/hero-style-examples/` are reference-only and are not approved production artwork.
