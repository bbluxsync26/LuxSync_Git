# Voice & Tone

## Brand Voice

Intelligent Calm

## Personality

- Warm
- Confident
- Thoughtful
- Unhurried
- Professional

## We Sound Like

A trusted advisor.

## We Do Not Sound Like

- Pushy salespeople
- Tech enthusiasts showing off
- Corporate marketing jargon

## Example

Bad:

Buy now before supplies run out!

Good:

Thoughtfully selected solutions designed to make everyday living simpler and more enjoyable.
