## Concierge Positioning

**LuxSync Concierge** is a flagship homepage journey. It should lead customers from lifestyle goals and property needs to recommended LuxSync Experiences and **My LuxSync Blueprint** before requiring them to choose individual devices.

The experience follows:

**Lifestyle → Experience → Intelligence → Technology**

Source: `docs/architecture/intelligent-living-concierge.md` and `website/src/concierge/`.

## Content Rules

- Use the Intelligent Calm voice.
- Keep LuxSync commerce-first while making guided discovery a primary customer journey.
- Use `content/product-catalog.md` for current product-family, bundle, and solution-concept language.
- Use validated product names, prices, stock, compatibility, and availability only.
- Do not invent ratings, reviews, partnerships, certifications, customer counts, or scarcity.
- Do not present SmartThings templates, LuxSync Grid, or other roadmap products as live before release.
- Route support to `support@luxsync.net` and general information/consultations to `info@luxsync.net`.
- Route **Get the ROI Guide** to the library at `content/guides/roi/README.md`, where visitors choose the guide that matches their role or household.
