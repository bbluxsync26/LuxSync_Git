# The LuxSync ROI Guide for STR Operators

## Where Luxury Lives Intelligently

**For the teams responsible for the daily operation of one or more short-term rentals**

STR operators live in the space between guest expectations and property reality. Their ROI comes from reducing repetitive coordination, improving exception visibility, standardizing property behavior, and helping teams act before small issues become expensive disruptions.

## The STR Operator ROI Case

| Value area | What to measure | Relevant LuxSync capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Check-in operations | Failed entries, manual code work, arrival interventions | Guest Ready, compatible entry/access |
| Turnover coordination | Readiness checks, missed steps, rework, extra trips | Turnover, Property Pulse, standardized routines |
| Exception management | Time to detect, assign, and resolve property exceptions | Property Pulse, Water Watch, climate/status alerts |
| Utility operations | Unoccupied lighting/climate runtime across units | Climate Intelligence, Energy Intelligence, Away |
| Support volume | Guest contacts about access, comfort, Wi-Fi, and controls | Clear guidance, compatible foundations, simplified experiences |
| Team capacity | Units managed per operations hour and after-hours workload | Standardized Blueprints, repeatable experiences, remote status |

## Establish the Operating Baseline

Track by property and across the portfolio:

- arrivals, failed/self-service check-ins, and manual interventions;
- turnovers, readiness exceptions, rework, and extra site visits;
- guest-support contacts by category and handling time;
- after-hours escalations and time to resolution;
- utility cost per occupied and available night where practical;
- device exceptions, battery/service events, and technology downtime;
- current systems, integrations, responsibilities, and local support coverage.

## STR Operator ROI Worksheet

| Input | Your value | Calculation |
|---|---:|---|
| Annual verified operations time recovered | $ | Avoided task hours × loaded labor cost |
| Annual avoided dispatch/site-visit cost | $ | Verified avoided visits × fully loaded cost |
| Annual verified utility savings | $ | Normalized baseline minus measured result |
| Annual reduction in rework | $ | Avoided repeated tasks × labor/vendor cost |
| Attributable capacity value | $ | Additional sustainable unit capacity × evidenced contribution; keep separate until verified |
| Risk-adjusted incident value | $ | Probability × attributable avoidable cost |
| Recurring and initial costs | $ | Subtract from benefits per period |

## Operator-Focused Pilot

Choose a small group of representative properties—not only the easiest units. Standardize the foundation, naming, access roles, routines, alert ownership, and escalation rules. Compare results by property and portfolio so a strong result at one unit does not hide poor adoption elsewhere.

Measure:

- intervention rate per 100 stays;
- turnover exception rate;
- guest technology contacts per 100 stays;
- after-hours operations minutes per stay;
- utility cost normalized for occupancy and weather;
- device uptime and repeat fault rate.

## What a LuxSync STR Operator Blueprint Can Include

- portfolio and property profiles;
- common foundation plus property-specific exceptions;
- Guest Ready, Turnover, Property Pulse, Water Watch, Climate Intelligence, and Away experiences;
- alert ownership and human escalation requirements;
- rollout waves and training guidance;
- portfolio dashboard requirements without claiming unreleased software is live.

## Important Boundaries

Automation should make responsibilities clearer, not remove local accountability. LuxSync does not guarantee labor reductions, operating capacity, reviews, revenue, or incident prevention. Validate guest privacy, access controls, camera/noise-device rules, and local law for every property.
