# The LuxSync ROI Guide for Commercial Offices

## Where Luxury Lives Intelligently

**For office owners, workplace leaders, facilities teams, and property managers**

An intelligent office should do more than add devices. It should reduce avoidable operating effort, support comfortable workdays, improve visibility across the property, and create a more responsive environment without adding technology friction.

## The Office ROI Case

| Value area | What to measure | Relevant LuxSync capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Energy management | Lighting and HVAC use during occupied and unoccupied hours | Lighting & Ambience, Climate Intelligence, Energy Intelligence, Away |
| Facilities efficiency | Time spent on repetitive checks, resets, and routine adjustments | Property Pulse, Effortless Departure, scheduled routines |
| Water and property awareness | Leak events, response time, affected area, avoidable downtime | Water Watch, compatible sensors and shut-off products |
| Space readiness | Time required to prepare meeting, reception, and shared spaces | Welcome, Entertain, lighting and climate scenes |
| Workplace experience | Comfort requests, environment-related tickets, employee feedback | Climate, lighting, shades, ambience, accessible controls |
| Multi-location oversight | Site visits, after-hours exceptions, visibility gaps | Property Pulse, remote status, standardized routines |

## Build the Baseline

Use at least 30–90 days of available operating data before comparing outcomes:

- monthly electricity and climate costs;
- normal business hours and after-hours usage;
- facilities labor spent on repeatable environmental tasks;
- comfort and environment-related tickets;
- water incidents, response times, and downtime;
- number of sites, floors, rooms, and shared spaces;
- existing SmartThings, Matter, lighting, climate, access, and network infrastructure.

## Office ROI Worksheet

| Input | Your value | Calculation |
|---|---:|---|
| Annual verified lighting/climate savings | $ | Baseline cost minus measured post-launch cost, normalized for weather and occupancy |
| Annual facilities time recovered | $ | Verified hours avoided × loaded hourly labor cost |
| Annual avoided site-visit cost | $ | Avoided trips × fully loaded cost per trip |
| Risk-adjusted water/property value | $ | Event probability × attributable avoidable cost; keep separate from guaranteed savings |
| Annual recurring platform/service cost | $ | Subtract from benefits |
| Initial equipment/setup investment | $ | Use validated proposal data |

```text
Annual net benefit = verified savings + recovered labor + risk-adjusted value - recurring cost
Simple ROI = annual net benefit / initial investment × 100
Payback months = initial investment / (annual net benefit / 12)
```

## Start With a Measurable Pilot

Choose one floor, department, or site where the baseline is clear. Prioritize two or three outcomes, such as after-hours lighting, climate scheduling, and leak awareness. Run the pilot long enough to account for operating and weather variation, then compare normalized results before expanding.

## Important Boundaries

Results depend on building systems, occupancy, climate, tariffs, operating practices, device compatibility, and adoption. LuxSync does not guarantee savings or operational outcomes. Access, security-related, and property-awareness technology does not replace code-required systems, professional monitoring, or emergency services.
