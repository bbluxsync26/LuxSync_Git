# The LuxSync ROI Guide for STR Managers

## Where Luxury Lives Intelligently

**For professional short-term-rental managers and management-company leaders**

An STR manager must deliver results for two customers: the guest and the property owner. The manager ROI case therefore includes service efficiency, scalable standards, owner confidence, portfolio growth, and the cost of supporting technology across unlike properties.

## The STR Manager ROI Case

| Value area | What to measure | Relevant LuxSync capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Management margin | Technology-related labor and vendor cost per unit | Standardized compatible foundations and Blueprints |
| Owner retention | Owner concerns, renewal reasons, reporting confidence | Property Pulse, documented performance and exception handling |
| Portfolio onboarding | Time to assess, configure, document, and train each property | Repeatable Property Profile and implementation paths |
| Guest service | Technology contacts, escalation rate, resolution time | Guest Ready, simplified controls, compatible products |
| Field operations | Dispatches, rework, after-hours interventions | Turnover, Water Watch, climate/status awareness |
| Portfolio scalability | Units supported per manager/operations team | Standards, roles, templates, phased rollout |

## Establish the Management Baseline

Track:

- active units, new-unit onboarding time, and technology assessment cost;
- management revenue and direct service cost by unit where available;
- technology-related tickets, escalations, vendor calls, and dispatches;
- owner questions, technology-related dissatisfaction, and contract-renewal themes;
- guest issue rates and resolution time;
- device and ecosystem variation across the portfolio;
- training time for internal teams, cleaners, vendors, and local contacts.

## STR Manager ROI Worksheet

| Input | Your value | Calculation |
|---|---:|---|
| Annual service labor recovered | $ | Verified avoided hours × loaded labor cost |
| Annual dispatch/rework savings | $ | Avoided events × fully loaded event cost |
| Annual onboarding efficiency | $ | Verified hours avoided per new unit × units onboarded × loaded cost |
| Attributable owner-retention value | $ | Retained contribution linked to evidenced decision factors; scenario until verified |
| Attributable scalable-capacity value | $ | Added sustainable units × evidenced contribution, net of added support cost |
| Recurring and initial costs | $ | Subtract implementation, platform, training, support, and replacement costs |

## Management-Company Decision Rules

- Segment properties by ecosystem, size, connectivity, and owner appetite before standardizing.
- Define a supported-product policy and exception process.
- Assign alert owners, after-hours escalation, battery maintenance, and device-replacement responsibility.
- Show owners measured facts, not implied guarantees.
- Keep guest privacy and platform disclosure rules inside onboarding and quality assurance.

## What a LuxSync STR Manager Blueprint Can Include

- portfolio segmentation and property standards;
- supported foundation and approved capability categories;
- minimum, preferred, and premium property packages using validated products;
- Guest Ready, Turnover, Property Pulse, Water Watch, and Climate Intelligence mappings;
- operating roles, handoffs, training, and exception paths;
- owner-facing measurement framework and rollout sequence.

## Important Boundaries

LuxSync does not guarantee owner retention, portfolio growth, reviews, labor savings, or management margin. Any capacity or retention value must be linked to actual operating and customer evidence. Security-related and property-awareness products do not replace emergency response, professional monitoring, code-required systems, or local property support.
