## Shared ROI Standard

All guides use the same core equations:

```text
Annual verified benefit
= measured cost savings
+ measured labor/time value
+ attributable revenue or retention value
+ risk-adjusted avoided-cost value

Annual net benefit
= annual verified benefit - annual recurring cost

Simple ROI
= (annual net benefit / initial investment) × 100

Payback period in months
= initial investment / monthly net benefit

Three-year net value
= total verified benefits over 36 months
- initial investment
- recurring costs over 36 months
```

Use only benefits that can be supported by baseline data and post-implementation measurement. Treat avoided losses, occupancy effects, retention effects, and time-value estimates as scenarios until evidence makes them attributable.

## Publication Guardrails

- Do not promise a specific ROI, savings percentage, payback period, occupancy increase, insurance reduction, or labor reduction.
- Do not publish unvalidated product prices, availability, compatibility, or bundle contents.
- Do not present convenience or property-awareness technology as a substitute for life-safety systems, emergency services, professional monitoring, clinical care, or required staffing.
- Nursing-home, senior-living, and residential-senior guidance must avoid medical claims and respect applicable privacy, accessibility, consent, and regulatory requirements.
- Use Samsung SmartThings as the primary launch compatibility standard; validate each device and integration before recommendation or sale.
