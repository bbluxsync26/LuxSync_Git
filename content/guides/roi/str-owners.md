# The LuxSync ROI Guide for STR Owners

## Where Luxury Lives Intelligently

**For individual and portfolio short-term-rental property owners**

For an STR owner, intelligent technology should strengthen the property's net operating performance and guest experience without creating a fragile collection of disconnected devices. The owner ROI case centers on property-level income, operating costs, asset awareness, and long-term flexibility.

## The STR Owner ROI Case

| Value area | What to measure | Relevant LuxSync capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Utility control | Energy used between stays and during unoccupied periods | Climate Intelligence, Energy Intelligence, Away |
| Guest arrival | Access issues, failed check-ins, owner interventions | Guest Ready, compatible smart entry and lighting |
| Property awareness | Leak events, temperature exceptions, doors/windows, response time | Water Watch, Property Pulse, Protect |
| Turnover readiness | Time spent confirming property state before arrival | Turnover, Guest Ready, remote status |
| Guest experience | Technology-related messages, review themes, comfort issues | Welcome, lighting, climate, entertainment, accessible controls |
| Owner time | Hours spent on repeatable checks and device coordination | Standardized routines, remote visibility, Blueprint guidance |

## Establish the Property Baseline

Collect 6–12 months where available, while accounting for seasonality:

- nights available, nights booked, average daily rate, and gross booking revenue;
- electricity, gas, water, and internet costs;
- owner time spent on access, thermostat, lighting, and troubleshooting tasks;
- guest messages and incidents related to entry, comfort, connectivity, or devices;
- turnover confirmations and emergency/extra site visits;
- water or property incidents and attributable costs;
- current platform, lock, thermostat, lighting, sensor, and SmartThings compatibility.

## STR Owner ROI Worksheet

| Input | Your value | Calculation |
|---|---:|---|
| Annual verified utility savings | $ | Seasonally normalized baseline minus measured result |
| Annual owner time recovered | $ | Verified hours avoided × chosen owner time value |
| Annual avoided intervention cost | $ | Avoided trips/callouts × fully loaded cost |
| Attributable revenue/experience value | $ | Count only evidence linked to the implementation; otherwise show as a scenario |
| Risk-adjusted incident value | $ | Event probability × attributable avoidable cost |
| Annual recurring cost | $ | Subtract from benefits |
| Initial investment | $ | Validated equipment and setup cost |

## Owner-Focused Decision Rules

- Evaluate each property independently before assuming portfolio-wide ROI.
- Separate cash savings from time value and risk scenarios.
- Do not count the same benefit twice—for example, a saved site visit and the same recovered hour.
- Confirm that access, privacy, noise-awareness, cameras, and monitoring practices follow platform rules and applicable law.
- Prefer compatible, supportable products over a larger device count.

## What a LuxSync STR Owner Blueprint Can Include
