# The LuxSync ROI Guide for Residential Homeowners

## Where Luxury Lives Intelligently

**For homeowners planning a first smart-home foundation, improving an existing system, or creating a phased intelligent-living roadmap**

Residential ROI is not only a resale calculation. It may include measured operating savings, time recovered from repetitive tasks, reduced service calls, earlier awareness of property issues, and everyday value from a home that is easier to use. LuxSync keeps financial outcomes separate from personal lifestyle value so the decision stays clear.

## The Residential ROI Case

| Value area | What to measure | Relevant LuxSync capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Energy use | Lighting, climate, and avoidable device runtime | Energy Intelligence, Climate Intelligence, Away |
| Time and convenience | Minutes spent on repeatable lighting, entry, climate, and device tasks | Welcome Home, Effortless Departure, Goodnight, Gentle Morning |
| Property awareness | Leak events, response time, second-home checks | Water Watch, Property Pulse, Vacation Mode |
| Service simplicity | Troubleshooting time, duplicate devices, unsupported ecosystems | SmartThings-compatible foundation, Blueprint planning |
| Comfort and ambience | Rooms/routines improved and household satisfaction | Intelligent Evening, Cinema, Entertain, Relax |
| Accessibility | Tasks made easier through appropriate controls and routines | Accessible Living, Night Path |

## Build Your Baseline

Gather:

- 12 months of energy costs where available;
- household routines and repetitive tasks worth simplifying;
- technology-related service calls, replacements, and troubleshooting time;
- water/property incidents and response time;
- current hubs, voice assistants, locks, lighting, thermostats, sensors, entertainment, and network equipment;
- rooms and priorities for the first phase;
- personal value of convenience, comfort, and accessibility—kept separate from cash savings.

## Residential ROI Worksheet

| Input | Your value | Calculation |
|---|---:|---|
| Annual verified energy savings | $ | Weather-normalized baseline minus measured result |
| Annual time value | $ | Verified hours recovered × your chosen hourly value |
| Annual avoided service/visit cost | $ | Verified avoided events × cost per event |
| Risk-adjusted property value | $ | Probability × attributable avoidable cost; show as scenario |
| Annual recurring cost | $ | Subtract subscriptions/services |
| Initial investment | $ | Validated product and setup cost |
| Personal lifestyle value | Describe separately | Do not force comfort or peace of mind into a false cash claim |

## Choose a First Phase

Start with one or two routines that happen every day and one property-awareness priority. For example:

- Welcome Home + Goodnight;
- Climate Intelligence + Away;
- Water Watch + Property Pulse;
- Accessible Living + Night Path.

Measure for at least one meaningful season before projecting annual energy results.
