# Contact LuxSync

**Smart living questions deserve intelligent answers.**

Whether you need help with an existing LuxSync solution, want product information, are considering a consultation, or simply have a question, choose the reason for your inquiry and the form will guide you to the right next step.

## Direct Contact

### Customer Support
For help with an existing LuxSync product, solution, setup, compatibility question, or order:

**support@luxsync.net**

### General Information
For product information, consultations, business inquiries, partnerships, or general questions:

**info@luxsync.net**

## Form Promise

The contact form should reveal only the questions relevant to the visitor's inquiry. Property information collected here should use the same reusable LuxSync Property Profile used by **LuxSync Concierge** and **My LuxSync Blueprint**.

## Confirmation

### Thank You

**Your message has been received.**

A LuxSync team member will review your request and respond using the contact information you provided.

Support requests may also be sent to **support@luxsync.net**. General questions may be sent to **info@luxsync.net**.

## Content Rules

- Use the Intelligent Calm voice.
- Do not require a full street address during an initial inquiry.
- Do not automatically enroll contact-form users in marketing.
- Keep marketing consent separate and optional.
- Do not invent response-time guarantees.
- Do not collect medical information through accessibility-oriented contact paths.
- Do not present smart-home convenience features as replacements for life-safety systems, emergency services, medical care, or professional security monitoring.
