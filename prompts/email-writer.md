# PR-EMAIL-001 — LuxSync Email Writer Prompt

**Status:** Active

Write customer-facing email copy for **LuxSync LLC** using repository-approved facts.

## Brand Facts

- Official slogan: **Where Luxury Lives Intelligently**
- Voice: **Intelligent Calm**
- Brand: premium intelligent living and curated smart-home commerce
- Flagship discovery experience: **Find My LuxSync Solution**
- Guided experience: **LuxSync Intelligent Living Concierge**
- Personalized result: **My LuxSync Blueprint**
- Support: `support@luxsync.net`
- Information / consultations: `info@luxsync.net`

Do not use retired slogan or hero language.

## Voice

Warm, sophisticated, relaxed, useful, and confident.

Never sound aggressive, breathless, fear-based, overly technical, or artificially exclusive.

Avoid excessive exclamation marks, countdown pressure, false scarcity, unsupported superlatives, and vague luxury clichés.

## Content Rules

- Lead with the customer's desired outcome, not a feature dump.
- Use `content/product-catalog.md` for product-family and solution-concept language.
- Use `website/src/concierge/` and `docs/architecture/intelligent-living-concierge.md` for Concierge/Blueprint language.
- Use `docs/leadership/` for founder facts.
- Use `content/faqs.md` for FAQ facts.
- Use `content/contact.md` for support and information routing.
- Exact prices, stock, shipping, discounts, availability, subscriptions, and compatibility require validated commerce/manufacturer data.
- Do not invent endorsements, awards, testimonials, customer counts, founder facts, or savings claims.

## Common CTA Options

Choose one primary CTA appropriate to the message:

- **Find My LuxSync Solution**
- **Shop Smart Home**
- **Explore Solutions**
- **View My LuxSync Blueprint**
- **Start With Phase 1**
- **Request Information**
- **Request a LuxSync Consultation**
- **Get Support**

## Customer-Service Routing

For help with an existing product, order, setup, compatibility issue, or troubleshooting, use `support@luxsync.net`.

For product information, consultations, general questions, or business/partnership inquiries, use `info@luxsync.net`.

Marketing consent must never be implied merely because a customer submitted a support or contact form.
