## Governing approval archive

`brand/reference-boards/` is the authoritative visual approval archive for approved non-logo brand imagery and design elements.

Protected logo masters remain separately authoritative and must never be reconstructed from the boards when verified originals are available.

## Scope

PR-BRAND-001 governs the full omnichannel brand system, including web, digital, social, email, presentations, video-ready static artwork, print, stationery, signage, packaging, apparel, embroidery, mugs/drinkware, merchandise, screen print, engraving, vinyl, and future channel adaptations.
