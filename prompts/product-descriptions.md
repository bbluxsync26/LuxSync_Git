# PR-PRODUCT-001 — LuxSync Product Description Prompt

**Status:** Active
**Canonical catalog:** `content/product-catalog.md`

Write product, bundle, collection, or solution-copy for **LuxSync LLC**.

## Brand Contract

- Official slogan: **Where Luxury Lives Intelligently**
- Voice: **Intelligent Calm**
- Positioning: premium intelligent living through curation, compatibility, simplicity, and experience
- Primary launch compatibility standard: Samsung SmartThings
- Flagship discovery path: **Find My LuxSync Solution**
- Personalized recommendation output: **My LuxSync Blueprint**

Do not use retired slogan/hero language.

## Product Philosophy

LuxSync does not begin with a wall of gadgets. Explain the customer outcome first, then the capability, then the compatible technology.

Preferred order:

1. **Experience / Outcome** — what improves in the customer's life or property
2. **What It Enables** — practical capabilities
3. **Why It Fits** — compatibility and use-case context
4. **What Is Required** — hub, bridge, app, installation, subscription, or network dependencies when validated
5. **How It Can Grow** — compatible next steps or related LuxSync Experiences

## Catalog Types

### Exact Product
Use only validated product facts from Commerce Plus or approved manufacturer/integration data.

### Curated Bundle
Describe the overall experience and list exact contents only when the bundle has been validated.

### LuxSync Experience / Solution Concept
Use `content/product-catalog.md` and `website/src/concierge/`.

An Experience such as **Welcome Home**, **Goodnight**, **Water Watch**, **Guest Ready**, or **Property Pulse** is not automatically a standalone SKU. Explain the outcome and capability categories without inventing components.

## Required Guardrails

Never invent:

- price or discount
- stock or scarcity
- shipping time
- reviews or ratings
- supplier relationships
- exact SmartThings/Matter compatibility
- warranties
- subscription costs
- installation requirements
- energy savings percentages
- security guarantees

If a required fact is not validated, say it must be confirmed rather than filling the gap.

Do not imply endorsement by Samsung, SmartThings, Airbnb, Vrbo, or another third party unless documented.

Do not make medical claims or represent smart-home convenience/security-related technology as a replacement for professional monitoring, emergency response, or required life-safety equipment.

## Standard Output

### Headline
Customer-outcome-led and premium.

### Overview
2–4 concise paragraphs focused on the customer's goal.

### Key Benefits
3–6 practical benefits.

### Compatibility & Requirements
Only validated facts; clearly note requirements or review status.

### Best For
Relevant property/customer use cases.

### Related LuxSync Experiences
Reference relevant Concierge Experiences where useful.

### Call to Action
Use one clear CTA such as:

- **Add to Cart** for validated live products
- **Explore the Bundle** for validated bundles
- **Find My LuxSync Solution** when the customer still needs guidance
- **Review My Compatibility** when existing technology needs verification
- **Request Information** when the item is not yet a live commerce SKU

Keep language refined, useful, and non-aggressive.
