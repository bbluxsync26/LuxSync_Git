# LuxSync Guides Page Blueprint

**Status:** Active design baseline
**Primary source:** `content/guides/roi/README.md`
**Official slogan:** **Where Luxury Lives Intelligently**

## 1. Purpose

Create a premium Guides landing page that helps visitors choose the right LuxSync ROI Guide and then continue to compatibility, setup, product-selection, automation, and FAQ resources.

The page should feel like a calm advisory library. It must not resemble a financial calculator promising instant returns or a gated download wall.

## 2. Hero

# Make the value of intelligent living measurable.

Supporting copy:

**Choose the guide designed for your property, operating role, or household. Each LuxSync ROI Guide helps you establish a baseline, identify measurable value, and build a thoughtful pilot using your own information.**

Primary CTA: **Choose Your ROI Guide**
Secondary CTA: **Find My LuxSync Solution**

## 3. Guide Finder

Start with three large accessible selection cards:

1. **Commercial & Care Environments**
2. **Short-Term Rentals**
3. **Residential Living**

Selecting a category filters or moves focus to its guide cards. Preserve a visible **View All Guides** control.

## 4. Commercial & Care Environments

### Commercial Offices

For office owners, workplace leaders, facilities teams, and property managers evaluating energy, facilities time, property awareness, and space readiness.

CTA: **Open the Office ROI Guide**

### Nursing Homes

For nursing-home owners, administrators, facilities leaders, and care-technology stakeholders evaluating non-clinical workflow, comfort, room readiness, energy, and property operations.

CTA: **Open the Nursing Home ROI Guide**

### Senior Living Communities

For independent-living, assisted-living, continuing-care, and senior-community owners/operators evaluating resident experience, staff efficiency, unit readiness, and property performance.

CTA: **Open the Senior Living ROI Guide**

Display a concise care boundary: **LuxSync solutions do not replace clinical care, emergency response, required staffing, professional monitoring, or life-safety systems.**

## 5. Short-Term Rentals

Keep the three roles separate:

### STR Owners

Property-level net operating performance, owner time, utilities, guest experience, and property awareness.

### STR Operators

Daily operations, check-ins, turnovers, exceptions, support volume, and team capacity.

### STR Managers

Management margin, portfolio onboarding, owner confidence, service standards, and scalable operations.

Use role-specific CTA labels: **Open the STR Owner ROI Guide**, **Open the STR Operator ROI Guide**, and **Open the STR Manager ROI Guide**.

## 6. Residential Living

### Residential Homeowners

General whole-home planning, cash ROI, time value, property awareness, comfort, and phased investment.

### Busy Professionals

Daily friction, interruptions, away-from-home visibility, energy, and technology simplicity.

### Intentional Parents and Families

Morning, departure, bedtime, nursery, family usability, energy, and privacy-aware routines.

### Seniors, Caregivers, and Aging in Place

Resident-led accessible living, everyday ease, nighttime movement, comfort, consent-based coordination, and property awareness.

Use a visible care boundary and keep the senior guide equal in hierarchy to other residential guides.

## 7. Shared ROI Method

Show the four-step method:

1. **Baseline** — document current cost, time, incidents, and experience.
2. **Pilot** — begin with a defined property, zone, routine, or cohort.
3. **Measure** — normalize results and separate verified benefits from scenarios.
4. **Evolve** — expand only after usability, compatibility, and value are demonstrated.

Include these equations as native accessible text:

```text
Annual net benefit = verified annual benefit - annual recurring cost
Simple ROI = annual net benefit / initial investment × 100
Payback months = initial investment / monthly net benefit
```

## 8. Download and Email Behavior

Every guide must be readable on the website without requiring an email address. An optional downloadable branded version may be offered.

If email delivery or marketing signup is offered:

- make the purpose explicit;
- keep marketing consent separate and optional;
- do not block access to the web guide;
- use `info@luxsync.net` for guide questions and consultations.

## 9. Guide Detail Page Pattern

Each guide detail page includes:

1. audience and outcome statement;
2. value-area table;
3. baseline checklist;
4. audience-specific ROI worksheet;
5. pilot and measurement guidance;
6. relevant LuxSync Experiences;
7. limitations and safety/care boundaries;
8. **Find My LuxSync Solution** or **Request a Consultation** CTA.

Use canonical copy from `content/guides/roi/`. Do not generate independent ROI claims in the page builder.

## 10. Visual Direction

Use LuxSync v3 and Plush Drift tactile illumination:

- Slate Navy and Dark Suede foundations;
- Pale Driftwood primary copy;
- Dusty Steel interactive and focus accents;
- Antique Rose Taupe and Warm Taupe Mauve for restrained detail;
- Champagne Rose Gold Metallic as a limited premium accent;
- Manrope 500/600 for headings, navigation, and CTAs;
- Inter 400/500 for body copy, worksheets, and forms.

Do not use lavender, purple, Montserrat, neon effects, generic SaaS styling, or unapproved ROI badges.

## 11. Accessibility and Trust

- Use semantic headings and real HTML tables with clear headers.
- Provide keyboard-operable filters and visible focus states.
- Do not communicate guide categories by color alone.
- Make equations understandable in plain language.
- Avoid tiny worksheet text and baked-in copy inside images.
- Identify scenario values clearly and never prefill fabricated performance figures.

## 12. Acceptance Criteria

The page succeeds when visitors can:

1. identify the correct guide for their role or household;
2. understand that ROI depends on their baseline and measured results;
3. distinguish STR owner, operator, and manager economics;
4. distinguish nursing-home, senior-living, and residential-senior needs;
5. open every guide without a mandatory email gate;
6. continue to Find My LuxSync Solution or request a consultation.
