# LuxSync About Page Blueprint

**Status:** Active website design baseline
**Company:** LuxSync LLC
**Brand system:** LuxSync Production Raster v5
**Content source:** `content/about.md`
**Leadership sources:** `docs/leadership/bridgette-beardsley.md` and `docs/leadership/sheldon-bardol.md`

## Page Goal

Build trust by explaining why LuxSync exists, how its outcome-first approach differs from a generic electronics store, and who is accountable for the company's technology, strategy, customer experience, and operations.

Keep this page personal and credible without turning it into a résumé wall or inventing founder facts.

## Recommended Route and Metadata

- Route: `/about`
- Page title: `About LuxSync | Intelligent Luxury, Thoughtfully Curated`
- Meta description: `Meet the LuxSync co-founders and discover how trusted curation, compatible smart-home technology, and Intelligent Calm make smart living feel effortless.`

## Page Sequence

### 1. Hero — Why LuxSync Exists

- Eyebrow: **About LuxSync**
- Heading: **Technology should improve the atmosphere—not interrupt it.**
- Supporting copy: Use the opening story in `content/about.md`.
- Primary CTA: **Find My LuxSync Solution**
- Secondary CTA: **Explore Smart Home**

Use a spacious Slate Navy or Dark Suede composition with Pale Driftwood copy, restrained Champagne Rose Gold Metallic detail, and a **production-approved architectural or smart-living visual** governed by `website/asset-map.md` and the current asset manifests.

### 2. Our Point of View

Present the core belief:

> Luxury is not complexity. Luxury is confidence.

Explain that LuxSync begins with the desired experience and then curates compatible products and guidance around that outcome.

### 3. What LuxSync Brings Together

Use four restrained pillars:

- **Trusted Curation** — fewer, clearer, more intentional choices
- **Compatible Foundations** — SmartThings-centered launch guidance
- **Thoughtful Automation** — technology organized around routines and outcomes
- **Intelligent Calm** — premium experiences without technical noise

### 4. Leadership

Use two equal-width founder profiles on desktop and one column on mobile.

#### Bridgette Beardsley

**Co-Founder & Chief Technology and Strategy Officer**

Use the Compact Biography from `docs/leadership/bridgette-beardsley.md`, with an optional **Read Bridgette's Story** disclosure or detail page linking to the approved full biography.

#### Sheldon Bardol

**Co-Founder & Chief Customer and Operations Officer**

Use the Compact Biography from `docs/leadership/sheldon-bardol.md`, with an optional **Read Sheldon's Story** disclosure or detail page linking to the approved full biography.

If approved professional portraits are unavailable, use a refined branded monogram treatment or neutral portrait placeholders clearly marked for replacement. Do not generate or imply photographic likenesses.

### 5. The LuxSync Promise

Close the story with three commitments:

- Recommendations begin with customer outcomes.
- Compatibility and limitations are communicated clearly.
- Technology should feel useful, dependable, and at home in the space.

### 6. CTA Band

- Heading: **Ready to create a more intelligent space?**
- Primary CTA: **Find My LuxSync Solution**
- Secondary CTA: **Browse Frequently Asked Questions**

## Responsive and Accessibility Rules

- Preserve equal visual authority for both co-founders.
- Keep titles readable without reducing them to tiny type; allow natural wrapping.
- Use semantic headings and native text for names, titles, and biographies.
- Provide meaningful portrait alt text only when an approved portrait is available; use empty alt text for decorative brand graphics.
- Leadership detail disclosures must be keyboard operable and announce expanded state.
- Avoid auto-rotating biography carousels.

## Content Guardrails

- Use the exact approved names, titles, and biography sources.
- Do not invent degrees, certifications, awards, employment history, customer counts, or years in business.
- Do not expose internal transition plans, salaries, forecasts, supplier terms, or unreleased product claims.
- Preserve Intelligent Calm: personal, confident, thoughtful, and unhurried.

## Production Visual Assignment

Use the exact horizontal LuxSync logo and a live balanced founder layout. Use founder copy from `docs/leadership/`. Do not invent portraits or use generated founder identities. If approved founder portraits are not supplied, use elegant non-person placeholder treatments rather than synthetic people. See `website/asset-map.md`.
